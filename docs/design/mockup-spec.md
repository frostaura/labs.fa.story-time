# StoryTime — Hearth design contract

Authoritative for palette, type, layout and copy on every screen. User-visible wording follows [`copy-glossary.md`](copy-glossary.md), which wins over any mockup string. Source of truth: [`Interactive.dc.html`](Interactive.dc.html) (canonical, interactive) and [`Storyboard.dc.html`](Storyboard.dc.html) (static, adds three details: Content-filters settings row, "No account. No card. Just stories." onboarding footer, "Ends gently. No cliffhangers, ever." player footer). Both are verbatim copies from Claude Design project `3b16b5fe-28c3-428a-87cb-39d3ec8443ac` (2026-07-30); open them in a browser next to the running app when QA-ing. Where this spec records a **deliberate reinterpretation**, the spec wins over the mockup.

## Direction

"Hearth": warmth with everything non-essential removed. **No cards, no gradients, no glow** (the only glow is the amber CTA hover/press halo) — typography, hairline dividers, one honey accent, enormous breathing room. A screen that whispers. Kid-facing moments may animate (staged fades, gentle scale-ins, the breathing CTA); nothing bounces, nothing is loud.

## Deliberate reinterpretations (spec wins over mockup)

- **Plain language, no weaving metaphor (founder instruction 2026-10-14: "Get rid of the Weave phrases. Something more elegant and simple so parents and kids don't get confused.").** Every user-visible "weave / weaving / woven / loom / thread / tale" phrase from the mockups is replaced by the plain words in [`copy-glossary.md`](copy-glossary.md): a story is **made** ("Make tonight's story", "Making {name}'s story…"), is **ready**, has **N minutes between stories** ("Next story at {t}"), and a failed one **didn't finish**. The mockups' weave strings are superseded wherever they appear; internal type names keep "Weave" until a separate rename sweep.
- **The affordance system: every secondary action wears a pill (founder pass 2026-07-31: "buttons like back, adjust and so on are very much not obvious — do some UI magic rather than the almost unreadable and easy-to-miss buttons").** Hearth is quiet by design and the implementation had let that slide into *invisible*: back was 14px `Faint` ghost text, "adjust ›" was a `Dim` tail on a Caption, "All →" and `UPGRADE →` were bare accent labels with ~16dp of vertical hit area, and the Player's stop/replay were unbordered Faint text. The direction is unchanged — no filled buttons, no cards, no gradients — but a secondary action now has a **container**: a 1px `Border`-stroke capsule, radius = height/2, **44dp tall (40 compact)**, 16–20dp horizontal padding, `Cream` or `Accent` text, pressed → `SurfaceRaised` fill + `Accent` text + the navigator's Light haptic. Shared styles in `Resources/Styles/Styles.xaml`: `PillButton` · `PillButtonAccent` · `PillButtonCompact` · `PillButtonAccentCompact` · `BackPill` · `RowValue` (a row's trailing value + chevron, Accent) · `TappableWhisper` (small tappable text, Accent). Rules this encodes, and they are binding:
  1. **`Dim`/`Faint` is never the sole signal that something is interactive.** Idle picker rows lift to `Muted`; a tappable whisper is `Accent`.
  2. **Every tappable clears ~44×44dp.** The settings switch keeps its 44×26 capsule but the control is 64×44 of transparent touch area; the sample ring grew 34→40; the Home avatar ring 32→40; the Player's seek strip 30→44; drag handles 36→44.
  3. **Every tappable has a pressed state.** Buttons via VSM; list rows and the play disc via `TouchBehavior` (`PressedBackgroundColor` / `PressedOpacity` / `PressedScale`) bound through a named page element, because behaviors do not inherit BindingContext.
  4. **Back is one pill in one place** — `‹  Back`, top-left of the header row — on Library, Parent settings, Content filters and Premium. It cannot live at the bottom: the progress mini-bar docks there. Onboarding step 2 is the one exception (no header row), where it sits centered under the CTA. The Player gains a matching `‹  Close` pill beside its drag handle, because a gesture hint is not an affordance.
  5. **A chevron means "this navigates".** Settings → Storage is read-only and therefore lost its chevron.
  Specific replacements: Home's summary line keeps its Caption but the recipe entry point is now a right-aligned Accent **`Adjust  ›`** pill (the whole row stays tappable); `☾ Kid Shelf` and `All  ›` and `UPGRADE  →` are pills; the progress sheet's stop/close ghost rows are pills; the Kid Shelf lock pill grew to the shared metrics with `Muted` text; the Player's Music·PREMIUM row is no longer dimmed to 55% with a Faint tag — the row's tag is `EyebrowAccent` `PREMIUM →`; the recipe shelf gained a visible `Done` pill. `GhostButton` survives only for non-essential footer text and is documented as deprecated as an affordance.
- **Quality analytics is removed entirely (founder decision 2026-07-31: "remove Quality analytics entirely").** The mockup's third Settings toggle, the `ISettingsStore.QualityAnalytics` member, the `tw.quality-analytics` Preferences key, the `SettingsViewModel` property/command and the toggle test are all gone; there was never a sink, an emitter or an endpoint, and now there is no seam either. `PreferencesSettingsStore`'s constructor deletes the orphaned key from installs that predate the removal. A test asserts by reflection that neither `ISettingsStore` nor `SettingsViewModel` carries any member containing "Analytic", so it cannot quietly return. (Root `CLAUDE.md` still carries an "Analytics is local-only and OFF by default" invariant — that line is now stale and needs a founder-owned edit; recorded in `docs/known-gaps.md`.)
- **Story visibility rules, and one notification that keeps every page true to them (founder bug 2026-07-31: "ensure that newly-generated stories show up on all the pages they are supposed to").** The rules, now explicit:

  | Surface | Making | AwaitingApproval | Ready | Failed |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | **Home carousel** ("Recent & favorites", newest first, ≤8) | no — the story in progress belongs to the sheet | **yes**, meta reads "waiting for you" | yes | no |
  | **Library** ("Every story") | **yes**, meta "making…", tap opens the sheet | yes | yes | **yes**, meta "didn't finish" |
  | **Kid Shelf** | no | **no** — see below | yes | no |

  The Kid Shelf stays Ready-only deliberately: an AwaitingApproval story has not been heard by a grown-up, and the shelf is the one surface a child drives unsupervised, so showing it there would route around the approval covenant. It lands the instant the teaser is approved.

  Ordering is part of the contract, not an accident: the Home carousel's **first card is the
  newest story**, and because `SyncInto` inserts it at index 0 while Android's RecyclerView
  anchors on whatever it was already showing, `HomeViewModel` raises `HeadStoryChanged` and
  the view snaps the carousel back to the head. Without that, tonight's story is in the list
  and off the screen — which reads to a parent exactly like "it didn't show up".

  The mechanism: a Core-level **`IStoryCatalog`** raised by `IStoryStore` itself on every `SaveStoryAsync` / `DeleteStoryAsync`, with `StoryCatalog.Dispatcher` marshalling to the UI thread (injected by `MauiProgram`, since Core cannot reference MAUI). `HomeViewModel`, `LibraryViewModel` and `ShelfViewModel` each subscribe and reload through a `CoalescingReloader` (one in-flight pass plus at most one follow-up — a single weave writes its row six times), and rows are reconciled **in place** by `StoryListItem.SyncInto` keyed on story id, so the carousel keeps its scroll offset and its snap-tick priming. Previously only Home listened, and only to `IWeaveHost.WeaveCompleted`; every other list refreshed solely in its page's `Appearing`, so a completion, approval, favourite or delete that happened while the Library or Kid Shelf was on screen left it stale until the user navigated away and back. Putting the raise in the store rather than in each producer makes the guarantee structural: there is no way to change a story without every list hearing about it.

- **Home "Recent & favorites" is a horizontal poster carousel, not list rows** (founder decision 2026-07-30, supersedes the mockup's 44px-thumbnail rows). Snapping CollectionView of 220×300 cards: poster art (radius 16, ~200h), title (Outfit 16 Cream, tail-truncated), meta line (Quicksand 12 Faint: "Series · Episode 4 · 9 min" / "One-shot · 7 min", amber ♥ appended when favorite). Newest playable story first; next card peeks to signal swipe; tap opens the player (approval state respected), long-press opens the share · favorite · delete menu. The "RECENT & FAVORITES" eyebrow gains the mockup's "All →" link (opens Library). The mockup's first-row amber ▶ is gone with the rows. Centered-card scale emphasis was considered and deliberately skipped — nothing moves unless the child moves it ("nothing bounces"). Kid Shelf STAYS a 2-col grid.
- **Poster art is the procedural parallax engine (2026-10-14; supersedes the v2 shape-formation contract and the multi-candidate critic).** The story engine emits only an enum-only spec (scene, time of day, mood, palette, up to three features, seed); `src/app/StoryTime.Poster` plans and paints four parallax layers plus a fixed sky backdrop, ambient particles and a fixed vignette. Contract, composition rules, research and the critique log: [`poster-engine.md`](poster-engine.md). Hearth anchors hold: the moon and stars are `Cream`, the only warm light is small `Accent` amber, and the top band of every poster is kept clear for a title. Posters now tilt on the Home cards and Kid Shelf tiles too (reduced travel, ambient baked), not only in the player.

- **Player poster parallax is lifecycle-hardened with a sensorless drift fallback (founder bug report 2026-07-31: "zero effect").** `ParallaxPosterView` starts motion on BOTH `Loaded` and handler attach (Loaded alone never fires for views inside Shell modal pages on iOS — the original silent failure), follows `Window.Resumed/Stopped` so tilt pauses while backgrounded, low-passes accelerometer gravity into per-depth translations (current travel, pivoted between far and mid, is in [`poster-engine.md`](poster-engine.md)), and when no accelerometer exists (iOS simulator) or a started sensor stays silent for 2 s (watchdog), falls back to a slow ken-burns figure-eight idle drift (~16 s cycle, dispatcher-timer driven — yields by construction). A fixed vignette overlay rides above the four translating layers. Screen §5 is no longer a page. Kid Shelf: the mini-bar shows but the teaser CTA hides in kid mode (the teaser flow carries approval — browse-only covenant). All motion ≤300ms eased, nothing bounces; ember pulse and shimmer pause when the sheet's page is off-screen or the app is backgrounded. - **Anticipatory haptics: feedback fires at GESTURE RECOGNITION, never after the work (founder pass 2026-07-31).** The seven-word vocabulary above says *what* each haptic means; this says *when* it fires, and it is binding. A haptic is the acknowledgement that the phone heard the finger, so it must land at the moment the gesture is recognized — **pointer-down for taps, threshold-crossing for holds and drags** — and always **BEFORE** any command, navigation, animation, menu population, sheet transition or data load. Anything the app does afterwards is latency the haptic has already covered; a haptic that fires after an `await` is feedback about work that has finished, which is not the same thing and reads as lag. Concretely: (a) every `Button` fires its press haptic from the shared styles via the attached `services:PressHaptics.OnPressed` setter (default `Light`; `Medium` on the weave CTA and onboarding's final "Start my 7 free nights", the two threshold actions) — never per-page markup, and since `Button.Pressed` is not raised for a disabled button, `IsEnabled` is respected by construction; (b) rows, tiles, cards, chips, radio rows, drag handles and grips use a `TouchBehavior` and answer on `TouchStatus.Started` (`PressHaptics.Light/Medium/Selection`, or `PressHaptics.Touch(...)` for controls built in C#), which also replaced the bare `TapGestureRecognizer` at those sites because a tap recognizer has no pointer-down; (c) continuous tracks (length, mix, seek) tick at **first touch**, not at the pan recognizer's `Started` — that arrives only after the slop threshold, i.e. first *movement*, which left the handle feeling dead for the first few millimetres. The command-level haptic that follows the same gesture is then **coalesced away** rather than removed: `HapticTickLimiter` gives Light/Medium/Selection their own short windows (90/90/60 ms) and a passing press-down call arms a longer `PressRefractoryMs` (350 ms) refractory that swallows the follow-up, while a *new* press is only ever gated by the short window — a new finger is a new gesture and always deserves its feedback. Terminal outcomes (`Success`, `Warning`, `Celebrate`) are never coalesced and always land last. Still never on passive scrolling, page `Appearing`, keyboard typing, per-percent progress, or a disabled control. Debug builds trace every call as `TWHaptics <Kind>@press` / `~coalesced` alongside `TWAction` breadcrumbs, so the ordering is provable from a logcat capture rather than asserted.
