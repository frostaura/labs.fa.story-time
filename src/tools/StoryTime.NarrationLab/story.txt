Mila's little boat bobbed at the edge of the quiet harbour. The lamps along the pier glowed like sleepy fireflies, and the water whispered against the wooden posts.

"Are you ready for an adventure?" asked Pip, the small grey seal who lived beneath the pier. Mila nodded and pulled her blanket close. "Only a gentle one," she said.

So they drifted out past the lighthouse, where the beam swept slowly across the sea… and back again.


The moon rose round and silver above the waves. Out on the water, everything was soft and still.

"Look," whispered Pip, "the stars have come down to swim." Tiny lights twinkled in the dark water, rising and falling with every slow breath of the sea. Mila trailed her fingers through them, and they tickled like cool little bubbles.

An old turtle paddled by and smiled. "Hello, travellers," he said. "The night is kind tonight." Then he sank down into the deep, as slow as a sigh.


At last the boat turned for home. The harbour lamps were waiting, warm and golden, just where they had left them.

Pip yawned a very big yawn. Mila yawned too. The boat bumped softly against the pier, and the water hushed them both.

And so the harbour fell asleep, one light at a time. Goodnight, Mila. Goodnight.
